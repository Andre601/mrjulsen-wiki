# Traffic Sign Post

**Traffic Sign Post** is a Block that [[Signs|Street Signs, Traffic Signs, Traffic Lights and all Street Lamps]] can attach to.

## Usage

Traffic Sign Posts can be placed vertically and horizontally by clicking the top or bottom, or the side of a block respectively.  
If necessary will the Post bent at a 90° angle to attach itself to other Posts or any of the aforementioned Blocks. It can also connect to multiple other blocks too.

The Traffic Sign Post is the only Block that a Street Sign can attach to.
