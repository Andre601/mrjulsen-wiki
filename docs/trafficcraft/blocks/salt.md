# Salt

**Salt** is a Block that generates naturally in the world.

## Generation

Salt can naturally generate in any Biome with a 15% chance per chunk. It will only generate underwater at the surface.

## Obtaining

### Mining

Salt can be mined with a Pickaxe of Stone Tier or higher, dropping between 5 and 8 [[Road Salt]].  
Mining it with a Pickaxe enchanted with Silk Touch drops the Block itself.
