# Signs

/// redirect | `Traffic Sign`, `Town Sign`, `Street Sign` and `House Number Sign` redirect to here.
///

This page covers the **Traffic Sign**, **Town Sign**, **Street Sign** and **House Number Sign** available in TrafficCraft.

## Usage

### Traffic Sign

The Traffic Sign can be right-clicked with a [[Pattern Catalogue]] to apply its currently selected Traffic Sign Pattern.  
Any new right-click with a Pattern Catalogue replaces the currently displayed Pattern on the sign.

### Town Sign

Right-clicking a Town Sign with a [[Wrench]] will open a GUI not unlike a [[mc:Sign|Sign GUI]]. It allows 4 lines of text to be written, with the second one being larger in height than the others.

### Street Sign

The Street Sign can only be attached to a [[Traffic Sign Post]] and when right-clicked with a Wrench, allows to add text to display.

Right-clicking it with a [[Paint Brush]] containing color will apply the color to the sign.

### House Number Sign

The House Number sign can only be placed on the side of a block. Right-clicking it with a Wrench opens a GUI allowing you to enter text.

Right-clicking it with a Paint Brush containing color will apply the color to the sign.
